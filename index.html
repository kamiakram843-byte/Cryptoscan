How to read potential movement

• Bullish: price above the 1h/4h moving averages with positive MACD; stronger when volume rises.
• Bearish: price below the moving averages with negative MACD; rising sell volume can reinforce weakness.
• Volume ratio above 1 means volume exceeds its recent average; it does not alone prove buying pressure.
• TP percentages are measured from entry. They are possible target distances, not a prediction that price will reach them.
• BUY/SELL/WAIT are rule-based signals, not personalized financial advice. Always verify liquidity, spread and market conditions.
