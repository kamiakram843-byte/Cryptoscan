BUY setup
1H low touches/crosses lower Bollinger Band AND 1H RSI < 30. A live 15M green candle moving upward triggers EARLY BUY; a completed green candle gives CONFIRMED BUY.

SELL setup
1H high touches/crosses upper Bollinger Band AND 1H RSI > 80. A live 15M red candle moving downward triggers EARLY SELL; a completed red candle gives CONFIRMED SELL.

4H trend
Bullish/bearish trend is calculated independently using EMA(20) direction and the latest completed 4H close. Trend is context, not a required reversal trigger.
