How signals work

BUY: completed 1H candle touches the selected lower band, 15M closes upward, RSI supports recovery, and enabled filters pass.

SELL / EXIT WATCH: completed 1H candle touches the selected upper band, 15M closes downward, RSI supports weakness, and enabled filters pass.

The terminal does not execute trades. A SELL signal is not necessarily a short-entry signal in spot markets.
