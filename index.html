How signals work

• BUY candidate: price rejects the lower Bollinger Band, RSI is below 35, and MACD histogram is positive.
• SELL candidate: price rejects the upper Bollinger Band, RSI is above 60, and MACD histogram is negative.
• Other conditions show WAIT. A trend filter uses moving averages. No indicator can reliably predict every price move.
• TP1/TP2/TP3 use 1R, 2R and 3R risk multiples. These are illustrative levels, not guaranteed to be reached.
• Spot scanner only. It does not place trades and does not need your exchange API key.
